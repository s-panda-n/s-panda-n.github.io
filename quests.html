        Backup and restore

        Everything is saved in this browser under sp.sidequests.v1, so your edits and crossed-off quests stay put between visits. They do not follow you to a different browser or phone, so export a file whenever you have made changes worth keeping.

        To change the starting 100 permanently, edit the SEED array near the top of the script in quests.html. Anything you add there that is not already in your saved state gets folded into the wheel next time you load the page, so the file and your browser stay in sync without wiping your progress.
